for people who get lost in their work

Never miss the start
of a meeting again.

A notification is easy to miss when you're deep in something. MeetingFlash turns your whole screen red right before a meeting, and keeps it there until you notice.

brew install --cask frugoman/tap/meeting-flash
Free and open source · macOS 14 or later · or download the app
// why it works

Impossible to miss, easy to dismiss.

It reads the calendars already on your Mac, so there's nothing to connect. When a meeting is about to start, you'll know.

Every screen, until you notice

The flash pulses on every display and stays up until you click anywhere or press a key. It won't fade away while you're looking at another screen.

Sound only where it's OK

Play alert sounds only on your AirPods, or only on your home Wi-Fi, so the office never hears your reminders.

One click to join

Upcoming meetings sit in the menu bar with a countdown, and Teams, Zoom, Meet and Webex links open in one click.

// alerts

A gentle nudge, then a loud one.

Add as many alerts per meeting as you like, each with its own flash and sound. A newer alert always takes over one that's still on screen.

  • At start, or 1 to 30 minutes before
  • Any system sound, or your own MP3
  • Skips all-day, declined and "Free" events
